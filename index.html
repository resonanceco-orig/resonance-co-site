From the Founder

A Story of Resonance

Events have been my work for thirteen years: eight as a planner, five in the classroom, and eventually as a researcher trying to name what makes some events resonate while others simply happen.

That question became my dissertation, and then it became Resonance Co. This kind of knowledge has lived in academic journals long enough; it belongs in the hands of planners who treat their work as craft.

If that is the kind of planner you are, or the kind you’re becoming, you belong here.
